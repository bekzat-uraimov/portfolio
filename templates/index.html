01About

I am a startup intern at akyldoo.ai, working on the Python orchestration layer for ThinkCoder. It is where I learned how agent systems are actually put together: LangGraph for the graph of steps, LiteLLM for talking to several providers, and why fallback and saved state matter more than the prompt does.

At ONER I build the platform: video delivery, local card payments, course structure, and an admin panel a teacher can use comfortably on their own. Six courses and 44 lessons are ready, and we are launching soon.

Away from code I design, and I still grade video. I think both come from the same place. I like it when things are clean and neat, whether that is a shot or a codebase. Keeping it that way as a project grows is the part I am still learning, and probably the most useful thing I am working on right now.

Associate degree finished. BS in Computer Science in progress, junior year at Bellevue College, with coursework in machine learning and data science.
